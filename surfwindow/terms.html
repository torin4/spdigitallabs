The short version: Surf Window's scores are estimates from forecast data, not a promise that it's safe to surf. Check the conditions yourself, know your limits, and follow local rules and lifeguards. Reports you post are public under your handle, so keep them honest and kind. Surf Window is free.

Forecasts are estimates, not safety advice

Surf Window scores each break by comparing forecast data with a profile of that beach. Forecasts can be wrong, conditions change quickly, and break profiles and hazard notes may be incomplete. Surf reports come from other users and may be inaccurate.

Surfing is dangerous. Nothing in Surf Window, including a good score, a level label or a report, means it is safe for you to go in the water. Always look at the conditions yourself before you paddle out, surf within your ability, and follow lifeguards, warning flags and local rules. You are responsible for your own safety.
