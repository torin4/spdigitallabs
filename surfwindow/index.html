Surf WindowWeb app · Free

Is it worth going, and when?

Surf Window scores live swell, wind and tide against the profile of each of 25 breaks in Shonan and Chiba. You get a number for every hour, and the reason behind it. In Japanese and English.

Screenshots

What it does

  • A score, not just data

    Each hour gets a score out of 100 for that beach, based on its own swell window, the wind it likes and the tide it works on.

  • The reason behind it

    Every score comes with a plain sentence saying what's helping and what's holding the session back: size, wind or tide.

  • Seven days, hour by hour

    Swell at the beach, period, wind, tide and wave power on one timeline, with the best windows of the week picked out.

  • See the swell window

    A live wind and swell map shows which directions a break can actually receive, so you can see why one beach works and the next doesn't.

  • Reports from the sand

    Surfers at the beach can report what it's really doing in three taps. Reports appear beside the forecast, under a handle, never an email.

  • Honest about danger

    Hours that are only for experts are flagged, and every break has notes on its hazards. Your location stays on your phone.
