Is it worth going, and when?

Surf Window scores live swell, wind and tide against the profile of each of 25 breaks in Shonan and Chiba. You get a number for every hour, and the reason behind it. In Japanese and English.

Open Surf WindowNo account needed

  • A score for every break

    Surf Window covers 25 surf spots in Shonan and Chiba. For each one, it checks the latest forecast for waves, wind and tide, and compares it with what that particular beach needs to be good. Every hour gets a score out of 100 and a short, plain sentence saying what's helping and what's holding the waves back.

  • See the swell window

    Every beach faces its own direction. Waves from some directions reach it, while headlands and bays block the rest. Surf Window draws this swell window on a live map of wind and waves, so you can see why one beach is working today while the one next to it is flat.

  • Seven days, hour by hour

    The forecast tab lays out the next seven days hour by hour: wave size at the beach, the time between waves (the period), wind, tide and wave power, all on one timeline. Surf Window also picks out the best times of the week for you, so you don't have to work them out yourself.

More

  • Reports from the sand

    Surfers at the beach report what it's really doing in three taps, shown beside the forecast.

  • Honest about danger

    Hours for experts only are flagged, and every break has notes on its hazards.

  • Japanese and English

    Every screen is in both languages. No account needed, and your location stays on your phone.
