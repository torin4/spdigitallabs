UnderstoryWeb app

A quiet journal for the slow work beneath the surface.

Understory is a private journal for grief, change, ordinary days, and everything that doesn't resolve on a schedule. Your entries are encrypted in your browser with a key only you hold. With Premium, a gentle companion helps you reflect, and every entry you keep grows a ring.

What it does

  • Private by design

    Entries are end-to-end encrypted before they're saved. Your passphrase and a recovery code are the only keys, so no one else can read your journal, including us.

  • Growth rings

    Every entry you finish becomes a ring, drawn like a tree's cross-section, oldest at the center. With Premium each ring takes the color of that entry's mood.

  • Seasons for hard stretches

    Mark an entry fallow for a low time, a nurse log for a loss, or windthrow for venting you don't want to keep, so it stays out of your rings and stats.

  • A companion that waits to be asked

    Premium's companion answers only when you ask: Root asks you a question, Tend walks through one thought in four steps, Glean offers a short passage, Seek answers briefly.

  • Heartwood, its memory

    The companion can keep one slowly updated, encrypted remembering of your journal. Read it, edit it in your own words, or clear it whenever you like.

  • Share with someone you trust

    Send chosen entries to a therapist or a friend with an encrypted link that can expire, and revoke it at any time. Save entries as a PDF too.
