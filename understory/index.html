UnderstoryWeb app

A quiet journal for the slow work beneath the surface.

Understory is a private journal for grief, change, ordinary days, and everything that doesn't resolve on a schedule. Your entries are encrypted in your browser with a key only you hold. With Premium, a gentle companion helps you reflect, and every entry you keep grows a ring.

Open UnderstoryFree to start · Works in any browser

  • Private by design

    Every entry is encrypted in your browser before it's saved. Your passphrase and a one-time recovery code are the only keys, so no one else can read your journal, including us.

  • A companion that waits to be asked

    With Premium, the companion answers only when you ask. Root asks you one question, Tend walks through a thought in four steps, Glean offers a short passage, and Seek answers briefly.

  • A word to sit with

    When you keep an entry, Understory reads its mood and offers a short passage and a few words for you, from secular or Christian sources, or both.

  • Every entry grows a ring

    Your entries become rings, drawn like a tree's cross-section, oldest at the heart. With Premium each ring takes its entry's mood, and your most frequent moods and themes appear beside it.

More

  • Seasons for hard stretches

    Mark an entry fallow, a nurse log for a loss, or windthrow for venting you don't want to keep.

  • Heartwood, its memory

    The companion can keep one encrypted remembering of your journal. Read it, edit it or clear it.

  • Share with someone you trust

    Send chosen entries to a therapist or friend with an encrypted link that can expire.

  • Speak instead of typing

    With Premium, dictate an entry and check its spelling without changing your words.
