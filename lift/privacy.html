Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Lift is a workout log web app at liftjp.web.app, made by SP Digital Labs, the name under which Joseph Suzuki-Parker, an independent developer based in Japan, publishes apps ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information Lift collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have.

The short version: Lift works without an account, and then nothing you enter leaves your device. If you choose to sign in with Google, your log is also stored in Google Firebase so it can be backed up and kept in step across your devices. Lift Pro, an optional paid plan, builds workouts with AI: what you pick is sent to Anthropic to write the routine, and payment is handled by Stripe. Lift has no analytics, advertising or tracking, and we don't sell data. You can export everything, and you can delete your account and everything synced with it from Settings.

Information we collect

If you don't sign in

Everything you enter is stored only in your browser, on your device. We can't see it. That covers:

  • Your routines: the exercises you add, with their sets, target reps, rest times and weight steps.
  • Your sessions: the date and length of each workout, the weight and reps of each set, sets you skipped, machine setup notes and any other notes you write.
  • Your body measurements: bodyweight, and any other values you enter from an InBody result sheet, such as body fat and muscle mass. If you choose to enter them, also your height and sex, which are used only to show the normal ranges for those values.
  • Your settings: language, rest time and your weekly set targets.

Like any website, the service that hosts Lift (Google Firebase Hosting) receives your IP address and basic details of the request when the app loads, and keeps them in logs for a limited time for security and reliability.

If you sign in

  • Your account: Firebase Authentication receives your email address, name and profile picture from Google and creates a user ID. Lift itself shows only your email address, in Settings. We never see your Google password.
  • Your log: the routines, sessions, body measurements and settings listed above are stored in Cloud Firestore under your user ID. Access rules allow only your own signed-in account to read or change them.
  • If you subscribe to Lift Pro: whether your subscription is active and a Stripe customer ID are stored with your user ID, along with a count of the workouts you have generated this month. Your card details go straight to Stripe; we never see or store them.
  • If you generate a workout: the choices you tap (goal, focus, time, equipment, experience), anything you type in the optional note, and the names of the exercises in your library, including ones you added yourself, are sent to Anthropic so its AI model can write the routine. Your sessions, weights, body measurements, name and email address are not sent. We don't keep a copy of what you send or of the routine, other than the routine you choose to save in your log.

Body measurements

Body measurements are information about your body. Lift stores them only because you enter them, and uses them only to show them back to you. They are synced only if you sign in. If you would rather they stay on your device, use Lift without signing in, or don't enter them.

Device and browser

  • Stored in your browser: your log, a copy of the app so it works offline, and, once you sign in, your sign-in and Firebase's own offline copy of your synced data.
  • Lift doesn't use your camera, microphone, location or contacts. If you leave "Keep screen awake" on, it asks your browser not to lock the screen during a workout.
  • Lift has no analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and it loads no outside fonts or scripts, with one exception: Google's Firebase code for signing in and syncing, which is loaded only once you choose to sign in.

How we use it

  • To run Lift: show your log, suggest weights from it, back it up and keep your devices in step.
  • To provide Lift Pro: build the workouts you ask for, and manage your subscription.
  • To keep your account secure.
  • To answer your messages and requests.

We don't use your information for advertising, and we don't sell it. We can technically open data stored in Firebase in order to run the service. We do that only to fix a problem you have asked us to fix, or if the law requires it.

Where it is stored, and transfers abroad

If you sign in, your data is stored in Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore), and the app is served by Firebase Hosting. Google may store and process data in the United States or other countries. The United States has no comprehensive federal privacy law equivalent to Japan's. Google commits to protecting data under its own policies and contracts, linked below. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. By signing in, you consent to this transfer.

If you use Lift Pro, Stripe processes your payment and Anthropic processes the request for each workout you generate. Both are based in the United States and may process data there or in other countries, under their own policies linked below. By subscribing and by generating a workout, you consent to these transfers.

Who we share it with

We share information only with the services that run Lift:

  • Google, which provides Firebase for hosting, sign-in and storage. Firebase privacy · Google Cloud data processing terms
  • Anthropic, whose AI model writes generated workouts (Lift Pro only). It receives the choices, note and exercise names described above. Anthropic privacy policy
  • Stripe, which takes payment for Lift Pro. It receives your email address and your card details. Stripe privacy policy

We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • On your device: until you delete it, clear your browser's data for the site, or remove the app.
  • Synced data: until you delete it or your account. When you delete a session or a measurement it is removed from the cloud, and a note that it was deleted is kept for 90 days so that your other devices remove it too.
  • Hosting and sign-in logs: kept by Google for a limited time.
  • Subscription status and Stripe customer ID: for as long as you have an account. Stripe keeps payment records for as long as the law requires.

Your choices and rights

  • Use Lift without an account. Nothing you enter then leaves your device.
  • Save a copy of everything with Settings > Export everything.
  • Delete a session or a measurement in the app.
  • Delete every routine, session and measurement with Settings > Reset all data. If you are signed in, this deletes the synced copy too.
  • Sign out in Settings, and choose whether your log stays on that device.
  • Delete your account with Settings > Delete account. This permanently deletes everything synced for your account, and the sign-in itself. If you can't sign in, email us from the address on your account and we will delete it for you.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have further rights, such as to see, correct or object to how we use your information, or to complain to a data protection authority. Contact us and we will help.

Children

Lift is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top.
