LiftWeb app

Log the set in one tap. Know the next weight.

Lift is for people who like to keep track of their training. Log each set in one tap, build your own routines, and follow your body measurements over time. Before every exercise it suggests a weight from your own history and shows you why. With Lift Pro, AI builds new workouts for you from the goal, time and equipment you choose.

Open LiftFree · No account needed

  • One tap logs the set

    Last session's weights and reps are filled in before you start. Tap the check and the set is logged and the rest timer starts. Change a number only when it changed. You can skip a set or a whole exercise, and Lift records it as skipped, not as a zero.

  • A suggestion that shows its reasoning

    Before each exercise Lift suggests a weight from what you actually did. Reach the target on every set and it adds one step. Fall short on the last set and it holds. Stay stuck for several sessions and it eases off. Tap Why to see the sessions and the rule it used. It is only a suggestion: type another number and Lift goes with yours.

  • AI builds the workout

    Tell Lift what you want: a goal, the part of the body, how long you have and the equipment you can use. Add a note in your own words if you like, such as an exercise to avoid. One tap and AI puts together a routine from the exercise library. Part of Lift Pro.

  • A draft you can read and change

    The workout arrives as a draft, not a finished plan. Every exercise comes with the reason it was chosen, and you can reorder, swap or remove anything, or change the sets and reps, before you save. After that it is a routine like any other, and Lift suggests your weights from your own log.

  • Every exercise, over time

    Pick an exercise and see its working weight session by session, with every set underneath. Stats counts your hard sets for each muscle over the last seven days against a range you set, which says more about a week of training than a total of kilograms does.

  • English and Japanese

    Switch the whole app between English and Japanese, coaching notes included. Lift can also show both at once, with the second language under each exercise name. That helps when your gym labels things in the other one.

More

  • Your own routines

    Build routines from a library of more than 170 exercises: barbell, dumbbell, machine, cable and bodyweight. Add your own exercises, and set the sets, reps, rest and weight step for each.

  • It remembers your setup

    Note a seat height, a pin position or a grip once. It is waiting on the same exercise next session.

  • Works with no signal

    Everything is saved on your phone first, so it works where there is no signal. A workout in progress survives the app being closed.

  • Opens like an app

    Add Lift to your home screen from your browser and it opens full screen, with nothing to install from a store.

  • Sync if you want it

    Sign in with Google to back up your log and use it on your other devices. Without an account, nothing leaves your phone.

  • Body measurements

    Enter the numbers from an InBody result sheet, or just your bodyweight, and follow weight, body fat and muscle over time.

  • Your data is yours

    Export everything as a file whenever you like. Delete your account, and everything synced with it, from Settings.
