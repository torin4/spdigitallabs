LiftWeb app

Log the set in one tap. Know the next weight.

Lift is a workout log for people who train on machines, one station after another. Your numbers from last time are already filled in, so logging a set is a single tap, and that same tap starts the rest timer. Before each station it suggests a weight from your own history and shows you why.

Open LiftFree · No account needed

  • One tap logs the set

    Last session's weights and reps are filled in before you sit down. Tap the check and the set is logged and the rest timer starts. Change a number only when it changed. You can skip a set or a whole station, and Lift records it as skipped, not as a zero.

  • A suggestion that shows its reasoning

    Before each station Lift suggests a weight from what you actually did. Reach the target on every set and it adds one step. Fall short on the last set and it holds. Stay stuck for several sessions and it eases off. Tap Why to see the sessions and the rule it used. It is only a suggestion: type another number and Lift goes with yours.

  • Every machine, over time

    Pick a machine and see its working weight session by session, with every set underneath. Stats counts your hard sets for each muscle over the last seven days against a range you set, which says more about a week of training than a total of kilograms does.

  • English and Japanese

    Switch the whole app between English and Japanese, coaching notes included. Lift can also show both at once, with the second language under each exercise name. That helps when the label on the machine is in the other one.

More

  • It remembers the seat

    Note the seat and pad positions once. They are waiting at the same machine next session, like the setup column on a paper record sheet.

  • Works with no signal

    Everything is saved on your phone first, so it works in a basement gym. A workout in progress survives the app being closed.

  • Opens like an app

    Add Lift to your home screen from your browser and it opens full screen, with nothing to install from a store.

  • Sync if you want it

    Sign in with Google to back up your log and use it on your other devices. Without an account, nothing leaves your phone.

  • Body measurements

    Enter the numbers from an InBody result sheet, or just your bodyweight, and follow weight, body fat and muscle over time.

  • Your data is yours

    Export everything as a file whenever you like. Delete your account, and everything synced with it, from Settings.

  • Lift Pro, if you want it

    An optional ¥480 a month. Pick a goal, a time and your equipment and get a routine built from the exercise library, with the reason for every exercise.
