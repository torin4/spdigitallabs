Delete your account

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Deleting your Lift account permanently removes the account and everything synced with it from our servers. It can't be undone.

Delete in the app

  1. Open Lift and go to Settings.
  2. Under Account & sync, tap Delete account and confirm.
  3. If it asks you to sign in again, sign out, sign back in, and delete within a few minutes. This proves it's you.

Your account is deleted right away, along with everything listed below. The same steps work in the iPhone app, the Android app and the website.

Can't open the app?

Email support [at] spdigitallabs [dot] app from the email address on your account, and say you want your account deleted. If you used Sign in with Apple with a hidden email, tell us roughly when you signed up instead. We will confirm and delete it within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign-in details. If you signed in with Apple, Lift's access to your Apple ID is also ended.
  • Your synced log: routines, workouts, body measurements and settings
  • Your Lift Pro record and your count of generated workouts

What stays

The log on your phone or browser is not removed, so you can keep using Lift without an account. To remove it too, use Settings > Reset all data, or delete the app.

A Lift Pro subscription is billed by Stripe. Deleting your account does not cancel it by itself: cancel it first under Settings > Manage subscription on the website, or email us.
