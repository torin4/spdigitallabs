Getting started

How do I start?

Open Lift. Sign in with Google, or tap "Continue without an account"; you can sign in later from Settings. Then tap New routine and add your machines in the order you use them. Or tap "Start from a sample machine circuit" for a ten-station routine you can edit. Then tap Start workout.

How do I put Lift on my home screen?

On iPhone, open Lift in Safari, tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen. On Android, open it in Chrome, tap the menu, then Add to Home screen or Install app. Lift then opens full screen like any other app.

Do I need an account?

No. Lift works fully without one, and everything is saved on your device. Signing in is only for backing up your log and using it on more than one device.

Does it work without a connection?

Yes. After the first visit Lift opens and saves with no signal. If you are signed in, anything you log offline is synced when you are back online.

Logging a workout

How do I log a set?

The weight and reps from last time are already filled in. Tap the check to log the set and start the rest timer. To change a number, tap it first.

How do I skip a set or a station?

Tap the set number to mark that set as skipped, or tap Skip at the top of a station to skip all of it. Skipped sets are recorded as skipped. They are left out of your averages and out of the next suggestion.

I closed the app in the middle of a workout

Open it again. Lift returns to the station you were on, with your sets, the clock and the rest timer as you left them.

How do I fix a past workout, or add one I forgot to log?

Open History, tap the session, then tap Edit this session. To add an old workout, tap "Log a past workout" in History and choose its date.

My exercise isn't in the list

Type its name in the search box when you add an exercise. If nothing matches, Lift offers to create it.

Suggested weights

Where does the suggested weight come from?

From your own log for that machine: what you lifted last time, whether every set reached the target, and whether you have been stuck at the same weight. Tap Why on the suggestion to see the sessions and the rule it used.

The suggestion isn't right for me today

Type the weight you want. A suggestion never locks you in, and next time Lift works from what you actually did.

The weight goes up by the wrong amount

Machines differ. Tap Edit on the routine and change Step for that exercise to match its weight stack.

Sync and accounts

How do I back up my log, or use it on two devices?

Open Settings and tap "Sign in with Google". Your log is backed up, and kept in step on every device where you sign in with the same account.

What happens when I sign out?

Lift asks whether to keep your log on that device or remove it from the device. Nothing is deleted from your account either way.

Someone else signed in on my device

If a different Google account signs in on a device that already holds a log, Lift asks before it does anything. It never mixes two people's logs.

Settings says "Not syncing"

Your log is still saved on the device. Check your connection, then open Settings and tap Sync to try again.

How do I delete my account?

Open Settings and tap Delete account. This deletes everything synced for your account, and the sign-in itself. For your security it only works shortly after signing in, so you may be asked to sign out and sign in again first. The copy on your device is kept; use "Reset all data" to remove that too. If you can't sign in, email us from the address on your account and we will delete it for you.

Lift Pro

What is Lift Pro?

An optional subscription, ¥480 a month. It adds "Generate with AI": pick a goal, a time and your equipment, add a note if you like, and Lift builds a routine from the exercise library. Each exercise comes with the reason it was chosen. Nothing is saved until you tap Save, and you can change anything first. Logging, suggested weights and sync stay free.

How do I subscribe?

Sign in, then tap "Generate with AI" on the home screen, or "Get Lift Pro" in Settings. Payment is by card on a page run by Stripe.

How do I cancel, or change my card?

Open Settings and tap "Manage subscription". You keep Lift Pro until the end of the period you have paid for.

I've used all my workouts for the month

Lift Pro includes 40 generated workouts a month. The count starts again on the 1st. Routines you have already saved are yours to keep and use.

Your data

How do I save a copy of my data?

Settings > Export everything saves your routines, sessions, measurements and settings as one file. "Import a backup" puts a saved file back.

How do I erase everything?

Settings > Reset all data deletes every routine, session and measurement on the device, and in your account too if you are signed in.
