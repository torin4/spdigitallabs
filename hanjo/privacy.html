Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Hanjo is a house design web app at hanjo.app, made by SP Digital Labs, the name under which Joseph Suzuki-Parker, an independent developer based in Japan, publishes apps ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information Hanjo collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have.

The short version: your designs are saved in your browser and, when you're signed in, to your account so you can open them on other devices. If you use the AI designer, the design you're working on and what you ask are sent to Anthropic's Claude AI service to produce its changes; Anthropic doesn't use it to train its models. Designs you share can be seen by the people you share them with. We don't show ads, don't use analytics, and don't sell your data. The AI plan is sold through Stripe, and we never see your card.

Information we collect

Your account

  • Email address and a user ID, whether you sign in with Google or with an email address and password.
  • If you sign in with Google, Google also gives us your name and profile picture. We don't show them to anyone.
  • We never see your password; our sign-in provider, Supabase, stores only a secure hash of it.

Your designs

  • Your workspace: your house design (site, floors, walls, rooms, doors and windows, stairs, roof, finishes, furniture and landscaping), the history of edits that produced it, saved versions and proposals.
  • Traced drawings: images or PDF pages you import to trace over.
  • These are stored in your browser. When you're signed in, a copy is also saved to your account so it can be opened on your other devices.
  • A design may show your plot's size, shape and orientation. It doesn't need your address, and we don't ask for it.

Sharing and comments

  • When you share a design, we store a copy of it, the email addresses of the people you invite and their roles, and the comments people leave, each with the commenter's email address.
  • For view-only links, we store only a scrambled (hashed) form of the link's code, its expiry date, and whether you revoked it.

The AI designer

When you send a request to the AI designer, our server sends Anthropic's Claude AI service, over an encrypted connection:

  • the design you're working on, as it stands;
  • your request, the earlier messages in that conversation, and what you have selected or marked on the plan;
  • which language you use.

Claude's changes come back as ordinary edits to your design. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, Anthropic does not use what we send to train its models; it may keep it for a limited time to detect misuse, as its terms describe. Our server passes the request on and doesn't keep the conversation. For each request we record which model was used, how many tokens it used, what it cost and when, to apply your plan's allowance.

Payments

  • The AI plan is sold through Stripe's Managed Payments, with Link, part of the Stripe group, as the seller of record. Stripe and Link receive your payment details directly; we never do.
  • We send Stripe your email address and user ID so the subscription is linked to your account, and we store Stripe's customer and subscription IDs, the subscription's status, and its billing period.

Device and browser

  • Stored in your browser: your designs and traced drawings, view settings such as the 3D options and the time of day, and your sign-in session.
  • Hanjo has no analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and its fonts are served from hanjo.app itself.

How we use it

  • To run Hanjo: save your designs, open them on your devices, and keep your account secure.
  • To share designs and comments with the people you choose.
  • To produce the AI designer's changes when you ask for them, and to apply your plan's allowance.
  • To process your subscription.
  • To answer your messages and requests.

We don't use your information for advertising, and we don't sell it.

Where it is stored, and transfers abroad

Your account, designs, shared copies and comments are stored with Supabase in its Tokyo region. The app and its server run on Vercel, whose servers may be in the United States or other countries. AI requests are processed by Anthropic in the United States. Payments are processed by Stripe, which may process data in the United States and other countries. The United States has no comprehensive federal privacy law equivalent to Japan's; these providers commit to protecting data under their own policies and contracts, linked below. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

Who we share it with

We share information only with the services that run Hanjo:

  • Supabase, which stores your account and designs and handles sign-in. Supabase privacy
  • Vercel, which hosts the app and runs its server. Vercel privacy
  • Anthropic, which runs the Claude models behind the AI designer. Anthropic privacy
  • Stripe and Link, which sell and bill the AI plan. Stripe privacy · Link privacy
  • Google, if you choose to sign in with it. Google privacy
  • The people you share a design with, or give a view-only link to, can see that design, and members can see each other's comments and email addresses.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and designs are kept until you delete them or your account.
  • Shared copies, comments and invitations are kept until you stop sharing the design; view-only links until they expire or you revoke them.
  • AI usage records are kept until you delete your account.
  • Stripe and Link keep payment records as the law requires.

Your choices and rights

  • Stop sharing a design at any time; this deletes the shared copy, its comments and its links.
  • Use Hanjo without the AI designer; nothing is sent to Anthropic unless you send a request.
  • To delete your account and everything stored with it, email us from the address on your account and we will delete it within 30 days. Any AI plan is cancelled at the same time.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have further rights, such as to see, correct or object to how we use your information, or to complain to a data protection authority. Contact us and we will help.

Children

Hanjo is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. For significant changes, we will also tell you in the app.

Contact

Questions or requests about your privacy: support [at] spdigitallabs [dot] app
