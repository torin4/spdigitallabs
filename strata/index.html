StrataWeb app

Read Scripture grounded in the history it grew out of.

Strata is a Bible reading app that opens every passage in four layers: what it meant then, what it carries always, how it turns to address you now, and a question for you to answer. It starts with Genesis, read in movements and daily readings.

Open StrataGenesis 1 to 11 free · No account needed

  • A library, read in movements

    Strata takes you through each book of the Bible in short daily readings. The readings are grouped into larger sections called movements. Each movement opens with an introduction that sets the scene and ends with a capstone that ties it together, so you always know where you are in the story. It starts with Genesis, and more books are added as they're published.

  • The history behind every passage

    Every reading starts with the history: who wrote the passage, when, and what was happening to the people who first heard it. Knowing a story was told during exile or war changes how it reads. Strata names its sources and sums up what scholars say in plain words, without long quotations.

  • Meaning, the turn, and your response

    After the history come three more steps. First, what the passage has meant to readers in every age. Then the turn: how it speaks to your life today. Last, a question for you to answer in your own words. Where the Bible holds different voices or open tensions, Strata shows them honestly instead of boiling them down to a tidy lesson.
