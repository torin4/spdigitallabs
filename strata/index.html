StrataWeb app

Read Scripture grounded in the history it grew out of.

Strata is a Bible reading app that opens every passage in four layers: what it meant then, what it carries always, how it turns to address you now, and a question for you to answer. It starts with Genesis, read in movements and daily readings.

What it does

  • Four layers on every passage

    The history (what it meant then), the meaning (what it carries always), the turn (how it addresses you now), and a response for you to write.

  • Grounded in scholarship

    Each history layer names its sources. Scholars are summarized and cited, never quoted at length.

  • Honest about the text

    Strata reads the Bible as a library written and edited over centuries. It names where the text argues with itself and doesn't reduce it to tidy morals.

  • Read in movements

    Books are read in movements and daily readings, with an introduction to each stretch and a capstone at its end.

  • A companion on every reading

    With Strata Plus, an AI companion draws out the meaning, the turn, and a question for each reading, made for you.

  • Your highlights, notes and journal

    Sign in to keep highlights, notes and your responses, synced across your devices. The Scripture text is the Berean Standard Bible, in the public domain.
