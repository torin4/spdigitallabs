Getting started

How is my daily allowance worked out?

Add your regular income and bills under Settings > Daily Allowance. The app spreads them across the month: whatever is left after your bills, divided by the days, is your daily allowance. Each day, the allowance is added to your Daily Balance and what you spend is taken off.

Why is my Daily Balance negative?

You've spent more than your allowance so far. It recovers as each new day's allowance is added. If it looks wrong, check that your income is set up as a recurring entry and that one-off income or bills are added on the right dates.

How do I change the currency or language?

Settings > Currency, and Settings > Language. The language can follow your phone or be set just for the app.

How do I add the widget?

On iPhone, touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit > Add Widget, and choose Daily Allowance. Tapping the widget opens a new entry.

Sharing a budget

Go to Settings > Budget & Sharing to invite someone by email or show a QR code. They join from the same screen on their phone. Everyone in a shared budget sees and edits the same entries. Sharing is a Premium feature for the budget owner, for up to 5 people.

Premium and subscriptions

What does Premium include?

  • Budget sharing with up to 5 people
  • Unlimited recurring entries (the free plan includes 5)
  • Spreading large purchases over several months
  • Analytics for every month
  • Full CSV export
  • AI reports and questions: up to 5 spending reports and 40 questions a month, resetting on the 1st

How do I cancel or change my subscription?

iPhone: open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Daily Allowance. You can also reach it from the Premium screen in the app.
Android: open Google Play, tap your profile, then Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions.

You keep Premium until the end of the period you've paid for. Deleting the app or your account does not cancel a subscription.

I paid, but I don't have Premium

Make sure you're signed in to the same Daily Allowance account you used to buy it, then open the Premium screen and tap Restore Purchases. A subscription can only be linked to one Daily Allowance account. If it still doesn't work, email us with the date of the purchase.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple or Google, not by us. On iPhone, go to reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, see Google Play's refund help.

Your data

Does it work offline?

Yes. Entries are saved on your phone and sync when you're back online.

How do I export my data?

Use Settings > Backup to CSV. The free plan exports the current month; Premium exports your full history.

How do I delete my account?

Settings > Delete Account. See deleting your account for what's removed, or if you can't open the app.
