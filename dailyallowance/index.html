Daily AllowanceAvailable on iPhone

Know exactly how much you can spend today.

Daily Allowance turns your income and bills into one simple daily number, then keeps track of the rest: what you spent, what you saved, and how this month compares.

Get it on the App StoreFree · Premium optional

  • One number for today

    Tell Daily Allowance what comes in, like your pay, and what goes out every month, like rent and phone bills. It works out what's left for everyday spending and turns it into a daily amount. The big number on the home screen is what you can spend today. Spend less and the rest carries over to tomorrow; spend more and tomorrow's number is smaller.

  • See where it went

    Each thing you buy goes into a category, such as food, transport or shopping. The Analytics screen shows how much went to each category this month and how that compares with last month. You can see at a glance where your money really goes, and which costs crept up.

  • Every day, over or under

    A calendar shows your whole month at once. Each day is green if you spent less than your daily amount and red if you spent more, and the shade shows by how much. It's an easy way to spot patterns, like weekends that always run over.

  • Reports in plain language

    With Premium, you can ask questions about your spending in your own words, or get a short report at the end of each month. The answers are written from your own totals, so they're about your money, not general tips. AI can get things wrong, so the app tells you that, and the reports aren't financial advice.
