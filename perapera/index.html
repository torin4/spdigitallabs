PeraPeraWeb app

Japanese practice for learners who are already good.

PeraPera is for adult learners from upper-intermediate to near-native (CEFR B1 to C2). You type the reading of every word before anything is shown, then use its AI tools: passages written for you at your level, a conversation partner to speak with, and detailed feedback on what you said.

Open PeraPeraVocabulary review is free · No account needed

  • Type the reading first

    Most vocabulary apps let you pick an answer from a list. PeraPera makes you produce it: you see the word in a sentence and type its reading in hiragana before the meaning appears. Words come back as cloze sentences and listening questions too, so you know each one in more than one way.

  • Find your level in two minutes

    A short placement test shows you one word at a time and asks for its reading. Get it right and the next word is harder; miss it and the next is easier. It settles on the level where words start to get hard for you, shown as a CEFR level from B1 to C2, and shows which levels are solid and which are still a reach. Review then concentrates on your frontier. Retake it whenever you like.

  • AI reading at your level

    Say what you'd like to read, such as a news article, a folktale or a business email, and pick a level and a length. The AI writes a passage for you on the spot, so there is always something new to read at the right difficulty. Tap any word to see its reading and meaning, then answer a couple of comprehension questions.

  • An AI conversation partner

    Speak with an AI partner in Japanese, out loud, about whatever you choose. Work through guided practice, or take a speaking test. Afterwards the AI reviews the session: what you did well, the gaps, the grammar to go back over and the words worth learning. You can also record a real conversation with someone, and the AI transcribes it and reviews how you did.

More

  • AI look-up

    Paste in a word, a sentence or a subtitle line and the AI translates it and breaks it down word by word. Tap any word to add it to your review, or type one in and let the AI fill in the rest.

  • An honest number

    PeraPera shows your reading accuracy over time. There are no streaks to protect.

  • On all your devices

    Sign in to keep your words and progress in sync across your phone and computer.
