Getting started

How do I open Tuck?

Press Cmd+Shift+V. Type a few letters to find a clip, then press Return to paste it into the app you were in. You can also open Tuck from its icon in the menu bar. Change the shortcut in Settings > General.

Tuck opens, but nothing is pasted

Tuck needs Accessibility access to press Cmd+V for you. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security > Accessibility and turn on Tuck. If it is already on, turn it off and on again. Until then, the clip you pick is placed on the clipboard and you can paste it yourself with Cmd+V.

The shortcut doesn't open Tuck

Another app may be using the same shortcut. Open Settings from the menu bar icon and choose a different one under General.

I hid the menu bar icon. How do I open Settings?

Open Tuck with the shortcut and press Cmd+, (comma).

Screenshots

My screenshots don't appear

Open Settings > General > Screenshots and choose the folder macOS saves screenshots to, usually the Desktop. New screenshots saved there are then added to your history.

To skip the files altogether, press Cmd+Shift+5, open Options, and set "Save to" to Clipboard. Every screenshot then goes straight into Tuck.

How do I find an image?

Type words that appear in it. Tuck reads the text in images on your Mac, which can take a minute or two for the first image after it starts. Type is:image to list only images.

Finding and pasting

How do I narrow the list?

Every word you type must appear in the clip. Add app:safari to show clips from one app, or is:text, is:file, is:image or is:pinned to show one kind.

What are the ways to paste?

  • Return: paste cleaned, without terminal color codes, trailing spaces or shared indentation
  • Option+Return: paste exactly as copied
  • Cmd+Return: paste wrapped in a code fence
  • Shift+Return: paste the text recognized in an image
  • Tab: mark several clips, then Return pastes them as one block

Turn the cleaning off under Settings > Panel.

How do I keep a clip?

Select it and press Cmd+P to pin it. Pinned clips are never dropped. A pinned clip containing {clipboard} is a template: pasting it fills in whatever is on the clipboard at that spot.

Your data

Does Tuck record my passwords?

Copies from password managers are skipped, and text that looks like a password or key is held in memory only and forgotten after 10 minutes. Add any other app Tuck should never record from under Settings > Privacy.

How do I delete my history?

Press Cmd+Delete to delete the selected clip, or use Settings > History > Clear History to delete everything except pinned clips.

Is my history synced or uploaded?

No. Tuck never connects to the internet. Your history stays on your Mac.
