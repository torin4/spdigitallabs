Getting started

What is the big number?

It is how far under or over you are today: the calories you have eaten, against the calories you burn in a day. "Under" means you have eaten less than you burn. Stay under, day after day, and you lose weight.

How is what I burn worked out?

From what you enter on the Profile screen: your sex, age, height and weight give the calories your body uses at rest, and your steps and workouts are added on top. It is an estimate. After two weeks of logging food and weighing in, the app compares it with what your own numbers show and lets you choose which to use.

How do I add food?

Tap the plus button. Type the calories if you know them, describe what you ate and tap Estimate, or take a photo. Foods you have eaten before appear underneath so you can add them again with one tap. Tap any entry in today's list to change or remove it.

How accurate are the estimates?

They are a reasonable guess, not a measurement. A photo can't show what is hidden or how much oil was used. If you know the real number, type it in.

How do I connect my health app?

On the Profile screen, tap "Use my health app" and allow access to steps and weight. On Android this uses Health Connect, so the app that counts your steps (such as Google Health, Fitbit, Samsung Health or Garmin) needs to be sharing with Health Connect in its own settings. If more than one app has recorded steps, you can choose which to use.

How do I add the widget?

Touch and hold your home screen, choose Widgets, and find Calorie Count. It shows your calories for today and a button to add.

Premium and subscriptions

What does Premium include?

Unlimited AI estimates from photos and descriptions. Without Premium you get one a day. Typing in calories yourself is always free and unlimited.

Why do I need to sign in to subscribe?

So that your subscription belongs to your account and follows you to a new phone.

How do I cancel or change my subscription?

iPhone: open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Calorie Count. You can also reach it from the Premium screen in the app.
Android: open Google Play, tap your profile, then Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions.

You keep Premium until the end of the period you've paid for. Deleting the app or your account does not cancel a subscription.

I paid, but I don't have Premium

Make sure you're signed in to the same Calorie Count account you used to buy it, then open the Premium screen and tap Restore purchase. A subscription can only be linked to one Calorie Count account. If it still doesn't work, email us with the date of the purchase.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple or Google, not by us. On iPhone, go to reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, see Google Play's refund help.

Your data

Does it work offline?

Yes. Your log is kept on your phone. Only AI estimates and backup need a connection.

How do I move to a new phone?

On the old phone, open Account, sign in, and wait until it says "Backed up". On the new phone, sign in with the same account and your log appears.

How do I delete my account?

Account > Delete account. See deleting your account for what's removed, or if you can't open the app.
