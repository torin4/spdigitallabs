Calorie CountComing soon

Calorie counting in one number.

Calorie Count compares what you ate with what you burn and shows you the difference. Take a photo of a meal and AI estimates its calories. That number is all it shows.

Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play

  • One number on the screen

    The main screen shows how far under or over you are today. It compares what you ate with what you burn. Stay under, day after day, and you lose weight.

  • AI estimates from a photo

    Take a photo of your meal, or describe it in a few words, and AI estimates the calories. You can also type the number yourself.

  • Two weeks at a glance

    A two-week view adds up the days and shows them beside your weight. Tap a day to see what you ate.

More

  • What you burn, worked out for you

    Tell the app about yourself once. It works out what you burn in a day from your size, your steps and your workouts. Steps and weigh-ins can come from Apple Health or Health Connect.

  • Checked against your own weight

    After two weeks of logging food and weighing in, the app checks its figure against your own numbers.

  • A healthy range to aim for

    The app shows a range to eat in each day. It is far enough under what you burn to lose weight, and not so far that it costs muscle. It tells you if you are losing weight too fast.

  • Your usual foods, one tap away

    Foods you have logged before appear when you add a meal. The ones you usually eat at that time of day come first.

  • Your log stays on your phone

    Everything you log is kept on your phone and works offline. Sign in only if you want a backup or to use a second phone. There are no ads.

  • Free to use

    Typing in calories is always free and unlimited, and you get one AI estimate a day. Premium removes the daily limit.
