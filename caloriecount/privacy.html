Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Calorie Count is a calorie counting app for iPhone and Android, made by SP Digital Labs, the name under which Joseph Suzuki-Parker, an independent developer based in Japan, publishes apps ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have.

The short version: your log lives on your phone, and you can use the app without an account. If you sign in, we keep a backup of your log so it is safe and can appear on another phone. A photo or description is sent to an AI provider only when you ask for an estimate, and we don't keep it. Steps and weight from your health app are only read, never written, and are never used for advertising. We don't show ads, sell your data, or track you across other apps and websites. You can delete your account and its data from inside the app at any time.

Information we collect

Your log, kept on your phone

  • The food entries you add: calories, an optional name, how it was added, and the time.
  • Your weigh-ins.
  • Your profile: sex, age, height, weight, typical steps a day and workouts a week. The app uses these to work out how many calories you burn.

If you never sign in, your log stays on your phone and is not sent to us.

Your account (optional)

  • Email address and name from the sign-in method you choose: Sign in with Apple, Google Sign-In, or email and password. If you use Sign in with Apple and hide your email, we only receive Apple's private relay address.
  • A user ID created by Firebase Authentication. We never see or store your password; Firebase handles it.
  • A backup of your log: your food entries, weigh-ins and profile, so your log is safe and can appear on another phone.

Health data

  • If you connect your health app, Calorie Count reads your step counts and weight from Apple Health (iPhone) or Health Connect (Android). It never writes to them.
  • It uses them only to work out how many calories you burn and to draw your weight trend.
  • Your average daily steps and your weigh-ins are stored with the rest of your log, and are included in your backup if you are signed in.
  • Health data is never used for advertising or marketing, and is never sold or shared with anyone else.

AI estimates

  • When you ask for an estimate, the photo or description you provide is sent to our server and on to an AI provider, which returns the calories. Your name, email and log are not sent with it.
  • We do not store the photo or the description.
  • To count the free estimates each day, the app gives each phone a random user ID the first time you ask for an estimate, even if you have not signed in. It holds no personal information. We record how many estimates you have used today.

Subscriptions

  • If you buy Premium, your subscription status: which plan, when it expires, whether it renews, and the transaction ID from the App Store or Google Play.
  • A one-way coded version of your user ID attached to the purchase, so a subscription can only unlock the account that bought it.
  • We never receive your card or payment details. Apple and Google handle payment.

Usage and device information

  • The app does not collect analytics, advertising identifiers or device identifiers.
  • Camera and photos: used only when you choose to estimate a meal from a photo.
  • Home screen widget: shows your calories from data stored on your device.
  • If you send feedback from the app, the email includes the app version and your phone model, which you can see and remove before sending.

How we use it

  • To run the app: work out what you burn, show how far under or over you are, and draw your trend.
  • To back up your log and bring it to another phone, if you sign in.
  • To produce an estimate when you ask for one, and to apply the daily limit.
  • To check purchases with Apple or Google and unlock Premium.
  • To answer your support requests and keep the service secure.

We don't use your information for advertising, we don't sell it, and we don't use it to train AI models.

Where it is stored

Your log is stored on your device. If you sign in, a backup is kept in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) in Tokyo, Japan. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Only you can read your backup.

Who we share it with

We share information only with the services that run the app:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore), which stores and processes data for us. Firebase privacy
  • Vercel, which hosts the server that handles estimates and checks purchases. Vercel privacy
  • AI providers, reached through Vercel, only for the estimates you request: Google (Gemini) for photos and Anthropic (Claude) for descriptions. They receive the photo or description and nothing else. Google privacy · Anthropic privacy
  • Apple, for Sign in with Apple and App Store purchases. Apple privacy
  • Google, for Google Sign-In and Google Play purchases. Google privacy

We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • The log on your phone stays until you delete it or remove the app.
  • Your account and backup are kept until you delete them.
  • The count of estimates used is replaced each day.
  • Photos and descriptions sent for estimates are not kept.

Your choices and rights

  • See and change your data at any time in the app.
  • Use the app without an account. Signing in is only needed for backup and for Premium.
  • Disconnect your health app on the Profile screen, or in Apple Health or Health Connect settings.
  • Delete your account in Account > Delete account. This deletes your account, your backup, your estimate count and your subscription record from our servers. The log on your phone stays until you remove the app. Deleting your account doesn't cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription; cancel that in your store account settings.
  • If you can't use the app, follow the steps on our account deletion page.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have further rights, such as to object to processing or complain to a data protection authority. Contact us and we will help.

Children

Calorie Count is intended for adults. It is not directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Not medical advice

The numbers in Calorie Count are estimates. They are not medical advice. Talk to a doctor before making significant changes to how you eat, especially if you have a health condition.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. For significant changes, we will also tell you in the app.

Contact

Questions or requests about your privacy: support [at] spdigitallabs [dot] app
